# Soulseek for Music, without Lidarr

Tracks are searched in FLAC on Soulseek through the slskd API. Without FLAC, an MP3 320 is offered to the user as an Accepted deviation. Lidarr is not used, for the reasons of ADR 0001: it files by album according to its own convention and would compute its own Missing items.

Nyaa has no music, public trackers have little FLAC and the good ones are private. YouTube only has lossy audio. Soulseek is the only public source rich in FLAC and is searched Track by Track.
